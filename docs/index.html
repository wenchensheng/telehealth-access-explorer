    About this app

    A phone-first explorer of Medicare telehealth use by state, rural vs urban, age and equity group, built as a healthcare analytics portfolio project by Chensheng Wen.

    Data source

    Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), Medicare Telehealth Trends. Public, aggregate data with no patient-level records. Coverage: .

    Definitions

    Telehealth-eligible users
    Medicare beneficiaries who received at least one service on the CMS list of covered telehealth services in the period, whether it was delivered in person or virtually.
    Beneficiaries with a telehealth service
    Of those, the people who received at least one of the services by telehealth (video or audio-only).
    Telehealth use rate
    Beneficiaries with a telehealth service ÷ telehealth-eligible users. This is the number on the map.
    Rural / urban
    From the beneficiary's mailing ZIP code using Rural-Urban Commuting Area (RUCA) codes.
    Dual-eligible
    Enrolled in both Medicare and Medicaid at any time in the period, a common proxy for low income.

    Limitations

    • Medicare fee-for-service only. Built from Part B claims; Medicare Advantage enrollees' care is not included, so states with high Medicare Advantage enrollment are represented by a smaller share of their beneficiaries.
    • It counts people, not visits: someone with one or twenty telehealth visits counts once.
    • CMS calls the data preliminary; recent quarters can change as late claims arrive.
    • Small groups are suppressed by CMS and show as "n/a". DC has no rural ZIP codes.

    Policy context

    Recent legislation extended many Medicare telehealth flexibilities, such as care at home and audio-only visits, through December 31, 2027 (HHS telehealth policy updates).

    How it was built

    Python cleans and checks the CMS file, SQLite + SQL compute the rankings and gaps, and this static web app (D3, no server) reads the exported JSON. It works offline after the first visit. Code: github.com/wenchensheng/telehealth-access-explorer.
